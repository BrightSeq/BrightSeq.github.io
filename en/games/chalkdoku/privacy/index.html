At a glance

  • Chalkdoku keeps your progress and settings on your phone. We do not have accounts and we do not run our own game server.
  • To play, you must accept usage analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase and GameAnalytics) on first launch. If you do not accept, the game does not start and you can uninstall it. Nothing is collected before you accept.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Where Google's consent form applies, it appears right after the Accept screen, and you can change your choice later in Settings.
  • Optional purchases are sold through Google Play. Google handles payments.
  • Chalkdoku is for players aged 13 and over.

1. Scope and who we are

This policy covers the Chalkdoku mobile game for Android ("the game"), support correspondence, and the BrightSeq website at brightseq.com. The game is published by BrightSeq, the game studio of 安徽明序科技有限公司 (Anhui Mingxu Technology Co., Ltd) ("we", "us"). Third-party services named below have their own privacy policies; we link to them in section 7.

2. Data stored on your device

The game saves the following on your phone: your progress (cases solved, ratings and stamps, the case in progress), your settings (sound, music, vibration), which tutorial and animation screens you have seen, and your choices in Privacy choices. This data stays on your device. We do not receive it. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may back this data up to your own Google account and restore it when you reinstall the game or set up a new phone; Google keeps that backup and we cannot see it. If backup is off, uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes your progress, and we cannot restore it for you. Hint counts you have bought are part of this saved data: they are not restored if you have no backup. Content you bought as a one-time unlock can be restored through Google Play (section 6).

3. Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics and GameAnalytics)

When you first start the game it shows an Accept screen. You must accept to play. Until you accept, the analytics and crash-report services below are not started and nothing is sent. If you do not accept, the game does not run, and you can uninstall it.

After you accept, the game sends usage events to Google Firebase Analytics and to GameAnalytics, for example which cases are played, whether a case was solved, how long it took, how many hints are used, whether an ad was shown or watched, whether a purchase went through (the product and its price), and which sound and vibration settings are used. The game also gives these services a random installation number that it makes up itself; it is not your advertising ID and it is stored only on your device, in a folder that Android does not back up, so restoring a backup on another phone does not bring it along. These services also collect technical data such as an app-instance or installation identifier, device model, operating system version, app version, language, approximate location derived from the IP address, and session information.

We use this to understand how the game is played, to fix confusing or too-hard cases, and to show our team how the game is doing. We do not use it to identify you, and the game does not ask for your name, email or phone number.

Data already sent is deleted automatically after the retention periods in section 8.

4. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

After you accept, the game sends crash and error reports to Firebase Crashlytics: the technical state of the app when it failed (stack trace, app version, device model and operating system, memory and storage state) and an installation identifier. We use it only to find and fix bugs. This is part of what you accept on the Accept screen.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob: ads between cases (interstitial ads) and optional ads you choose to watch for a reward. To show ads, AdMob and its partners may use your advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interaction data, and may store or read information on your device. Whether ads are personalized depends on your choices and on where you live.

Right after the Accept screen, the game may show a consent form provided by Google (User Messaging Platform). Google decides whether the form is needed where you are. In the form you can consent or open "Manage options". Your choice affects how ads are personalized and what data is used for ads. You can review or change it in Settings → Privacy choices, which appears only where Google requires it. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

We may also use Google's advertising measurement tools (for example linking Firebase and AdMob data with Google Ads) to understand which of our own ads led to installs. This uses data described in this policy and shares it with Google.

We do not sell your data and we do not receive your personal data from AdMob. We see aggregate reports about ad performance and earnings.

6. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional in-app purchases, for example hint packs, removing ads between cases, or additional content, are processed by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. The game receives a purchase confirmation (product, a purchase token and its status) from Google to unlock what you bought and to restore it if you reinstall the game. Removing ads removes the ads between cases; ads you choose to watch for a reward remain available. If Google refunds a purchase, the item it unlocked may be removed. Refunds and payment questions are handled by Google Play.

8. How long data is kept, and deleting it

  • Data on your device: until you uninstall the game or clear its data (see section 2 for Android backup).
  • Firebase Analytics: user-level and event-level data is kept for 2 months and then deleted automatically. Aggregated reports that cannot identify you may be kept longer.
  • GameAnalytics: kept under GameAnalytics' own retention policy.
  • Crash reports: deleted by Google after 90 days.
  • Advertising and purchase data: kept by Google under its own policies.
  • Support emails: kept as long as needed to answer you and fix the problem, then deleted.

We do not keep your gameplay data on any server of our own. Because we cannot identify you from analytics or crash data, we usually cannot find one person's records; to ask for deletion, email us at the address above and we will tell you what we can do.

9. Your choices and rights

You can: review or change your advertising consent in Settings → Privacy choices (where it appears); reset or limit your advertising ID in Android settings; and uninstall the game. Usage analytics and crash reports are required to play, so the way to stop them is to stop using the game and uninstall it. Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK, California and other US states), you may also have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. To make a request, email mingxufortune@gmail.com. We will reply within the time the law requires.

We treat players everywhere the same way: the same consent steps, the same choices and the same contact address. Where the EEA or UK data protection law applies, we rely on your consent for usage analytics, crash reports and, where Google's form applies, personalized ads, and on our legitimate interest in keeping the game working and secure; you may withdraw consent at any time without affecting what happened before. We do not sell your personal data. Where US state privacy laws apply (for example California), ad personalization by Google may count as sharing for targeted advertising; you can limit it through Settings → Privacy choices (where it appears) or by resetting or limiting your advertising ID in Android settings. We do not discriminate against players who use these rights.

10. Intended audience

Chalkdoku is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, contact us and we will help remove any data we can.

11. Security

Data on your device is protected by Android. Data sent to Google services is protected in transit by encryption. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Website and external links

The BrightSeq website is plain static pages. It does not use cookies, analytics or advertising of its own. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visits under its own privacy policy. Links to other sites are covered by their own policies.

13. Changes to this policy

When the game's data practices change, we update this page, its revision number and the "Last updated" date. If the change needs your choice (for example a new kind of data collection), the game asks you again. Significant changes will also be shown in the game.
