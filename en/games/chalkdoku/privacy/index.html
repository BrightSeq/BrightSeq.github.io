At a glance

  • Chalkdoku keeps your puzzle progress and preferences on your device. We do not have player accounts and we do not provide cloud save synchronization.
  • Before playing, you must read and tap Accept for this Privacy Policy on the first-launch screen. Closing the screen or reading the policy does not accept it. Nothing is collected before you accept.
  • After you accept, the game sends usage statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase and GameAnalytics, and crash diagnostics to Firebase Crashlytics. Once accepted, there is no separate in-game switch to turn off ordinary statistics or crash reports while continuing to play.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob (between cases and optional rewarded ads for hints) and uses Google Ads to measure installs from our advertising campaigns. Where Google's consent form applies, it appears after Accept, and you can review your advertising choices in Settings → More Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Optional in-app purchases are processed through Google Play. We do not see your payment-card details.
  • Chalkdoku is intended for players aged 13 and over.

1. Scope and who we are

This policy covers the Chalkdoku mobile game for Android (package: com.brightseq.chalkdoku, including test builds), related support correspondence, and the BrightSeq website at brightseq.com. Chalkdoku is published by BrightSeq, the game studio of Anhui Mingxu Technology Co., Ltd ("we", "us", "our"), which is responsible for the processing described in this policy.

Personal information includes data that can identify you directly or together with other information. Device identifiers, installation or app-instance identifiers, and IP addresses may qualify even when we do not know your name. Third-party services listed in section 7 also have their own privacy notices.

2. Data stored on your device

The game saves the following on your device so you can play and keep your choices: your progress (solved cases, ratings and stamps, the case or challenge in progress, and unlocked content), your settings (sound, vibration, language preference, and tutorial or animation flags), your hint balance, and your privacy Accept record (including the policy revision and text hash).

We do not upload complete saved puzzles, board placements, notes, or answers to a game server. If you have Android automatic backup enabled, Android may back up saved game data to your own Google account and restore it when you reinstall or switch devices; Google manages that backup and we cannot access it. If backup is off, uninstalling the game or clearing its storage removes your local progress and preferences, including any remaining hint balance, and we cannot restore them. One-time non-consumable unlocks (such as Remove Ads) can be restored through Google Play (section 6).

The complete text of this Privacy Policy is bundled with the app for offline reading. Reading it does not contact a policy server.

3. Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics and GameAnalytics)

When you first start Chalkdoku, it displays an Accept screen. You must tap Accept to play. Before you accept, the analytics and crash-reporting services below are not initialized and no events are sent. If you do not accept, the game does not start and you may close or uninstall it.

After you accept, the game sends usage statistics to two analytics services:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase: receives gameplay and feature events (such as starting, completing, or leaving a case, round, active play time, rank, hint usage, rewarded and interstitial ad steps, in-app purchase outcomes including product ID and local store price/currency, tutorial steps, and sound/vibration setting changes). Firebase also collects an app-instance identifier, automatic lifecycle/session events, app version, device model, operating-system version, language, Play install-referral data, and approximate location derived from your IP address. In our Android configuration, Firebase Analytics is set not to collect the Android advertising ID or Android ID for ordinary analytics, and Firebase ad-storage, ad-user-data, and ad-personalization signals stay denied by default.
  • GameAnalytics: receives gameplay, ad-step, shop-step, tutorial, and setting events via HTTPS from the game (without a native GameAnalytics SDK). The game sends a random installation identifier generated by Chalkdoku itself (an internal install ID), which is stored in the app's cache folder (excluded from Android automatic backup so restoring a backup onto another phone does not carry it over, though clearing cache resets it). The game also sends random session identifiers, session counts, timestamps, app version, device manufacturer and model, and OS version, and GameAnalytics' HTTPS endpoint receives your IP address (from which approximate country/location may be derived). We do not send the Android advertising ID, App Set ID, or crash reports to GameAnalytics.

We do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number in gameplay, and we do not send puzzle answers, board inputs, full saves, order IDs, or purchase tokens to analytics. Because installation and app-instance identifiers can link gameplay across sessions, we do not describe analytics records as anonymous merely because no account is required.

We use these statistics to understand how cases are played, balance difficulty, fix confusing steps, and measure product performance. Once accepted, there is no separate in-game toggle to disable ordinary analytics while continuing to play; to stop future collection, stop using the game and clear its storage or uninstall it.

4. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

After you accept, the game enables Firebase Crashlytics (including native/NDK crash reporting and Firebase Sessions) to investigate crashes, freezes, and technical errors. Reports can include crash time, stack traces, native crash dumps, app version, device model, OS version, memory and storage state, a Crashlytics installation UUID, and—where analytics is active—preceding analytics breadcrumbs or scrubbed script error summaries. We do not intentionally attach your name, email address, player inputs, or saved-game contents.

Crash reporting is covered by the same first-launch Accept and has no separate in-game toggle. Reports already uploaded or in transit cannot be recalled by uninstalling.

5. Advertising and user acquisition (Google AdMob and Google Ads)

The game displays ads through Google AdMob: interstitial ads between cases (after introductory free cases are completed, unless you have purchased Remove Ads or watched a rewarded ad during that case) and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch for a hint. Watching a rewarded ad does not itself grant consent to personalized advertising or unrelated tracking.

To deliver, cap frequency, measure ads, and prevent fraud, AdMob and its advertising partners may process your Android advertising ID, App Set ID, IP address (and inferred general location), device and app information, ad interactions (launches, views, clicks), and diagnostic/performance metrics, and may read or store data on your device. Whether ads are personalized depends on your region, device settings, and consent choices. Non-personalized or limited ads still rely on contextual and technical data for delivery, reporting, frequency control, and fraud prevention.

After the Accept screen, where required by regional rules, the game presents Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form before requesting ads. Depending on your region and Google's form configuration, you can consent, decline where offered, or open "Manage options" to save partial or non-personalized choices. You can review or change your advertising choices later in Settings → More Settings → Privacy choices (shown where Google UMP requires an entry point), and you can reset or limit your advertising ID in Android system settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Changing ad choices stops future personalized requests as applicable, does not cancel your first-launch Accept, and does not automatically erase data already sent to ad partners.

We also use Google Ads (together with Play Install Referrer, Android attribution interfaces, and Google advertising measurement integrations where enabled) to promote Chalkdoku and measure how many installs and conversions our own ad campaigns generate. We do not sell your saved-game files, and we do not receive individual players' personal profiles from AdMob—only aggregate performance and revenue reports.

6. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional in-app purchases—currently the one-time Remove Ads unlock (which removes interstitial ads between cases while optional rewarded ads for hints remain available)—are processed by Google Play. Google shows the local price and currency before you pay and handles all payment details; we never see your card or bank details.

The game receives purchase confirmation data (product ID, purchase status, and purchase token) locally on your device solely to grant the item, restore non-consumable entitlements when you reinstall or use Restore Purchases, and revoke access if Google Play confirms a refund. If consumable items (such as hint packs) are offered in a version, unused balances are stored locally on your device and are not restored after a clean reinstall without a system backup. Order IDs and purchase tokens stay on your device and are not sent to analytics. Refunds and billing questions are handled under Google Play's policies.

8. How long data is kept, and deleting it

  • Data on your device: kept until you clear Chalkdoku's storage or uninstall the game (see section 2 regarding Android automatic backup).
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: configured to retain user-level and event-level data for 2 months, after which expired data is deleted automatically on Google's schedule. Standard aggregate reports may be kept longer.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Google documents a 90-day retention period for crash traces, extracted native-crash data, and associated identifiers before removal from live and backup systems begins.
  • GameAnalytics: retained separately under GameAnalytics' own service terms and retention schedules (see https://docs.gameanalytics.com/event-tracking-and-integrations/data-retention-and-limits/data-retention-practices/), which are independent of Firebase's 2-month setting.
  • AdMob, Google Ads, and Google Play: retained by Google and its advertising partners under their respective policies for delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, accounting, and legal compliance.
  • Support correspondence: kept only as long as needed to handle your inquiry or privacy request and meet legal obligations, then deleted or anonymized.

Uninstalling the game or clearing local storage does not automatically delete data already sent to Google or GameAnalytics. Because Chalkdoku has no player accounts, an email address alone generally cannot identify your installation's analytics or crash records. To ask about deletion or access, contact us at the email below and include helpful context (such as device model, app version, and approximate dates); we will handle identifiable records we control and explain any technical or legal limitations.

9. Your choices, legal bases, and rights

You can:

  • Review or change advertising consent in Settings → More Settings → Privacy choices (where available under Google UMP).
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android system settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Stop future in-app analytics and crash collection by stopping use of the game and clearing its storage or uninstalling it.

Where applicable data-protection law requires a legal basis (such as in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland), we rely on: your explicit consent (on first launch for analytics and crash reports, and via UMP where required for advertising/personalized ads); performance of a service where needed to deliver a purchase or handle support you request; legal obligations where retention or disclosure is required by law; and legitimate interests (such as security, fraud prevention, and answering support messages) only where permitted and not overridden by your rights or where consent is legally required. You may withdraw consent at any time for future processing without affecting prior lawful processing.

Depending on where you live—including the EEA, the UK, Switzerland, California and other US states, or other regions with privacy laws—you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal data, restrict or object to processing, opt out of targeted advertising or "selling"/"sharing" of personal information (which may apply to ad-partner data transfers under certain US state laws), appeal a decision, and lodge a complaint with a supervisory data-protection authority (such as your local EEA authority or the UK ICO). To make a request, email mingxufortune@gmail.com with the subject "Chalkdoku Privacy Request". We will respond within legally required deadlines and will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

10. Intended audience

Chalkdoku is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. Store age/content ratings measure content suitability separately and do not replace privacy consent rules. Players aged 13 and over may still be minors under local law; where parental or guardian permission is required for data processing or personalized ads, please obtain that permission before accepting or using the game. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through Chalkdoku, email mingxufortune@gmail.com and we will investigate and delete identifiable records where required.

11. Security

Local data relies on Android's application-sandbox and device protections. Network communications with Google services and the GameAnalytics Collection API use encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections. No storage or transmission method is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please do not include sensitive details such as passwords or identity documents in support emails.

12. Website and external links

The BrightSeq website (brightseq.com) consists of static pages that do not use visitor analytics scripts, advertising, or tracking cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses and HTTP metadata for hosting and security under its own Privacy Statement. External links (such as store pages or third-party privacy policies) are governed by their respective providers.

13. Changes to this policy

When the game's data practices or service providers change, we update this policy, its revision number, and the "Last updated" date. Because Chalkdoku binds your first-launch Accept to the hash of the bundled policy text, an app update carrying a revised policy asks you to review and Accept again before continuing. Publishing changes on the website alone does not alter the behavior of an already-installed version or grant consent without your action.
