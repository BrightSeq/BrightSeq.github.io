Chalkdoku
A school at night.

A detective Sudoku set among the seven mysteries of a haunted school, drawn in chalk on a green blackboard.

How it plays

Every case is a grid. Each row and each column holds exactly one person, and the ghost counts as one of them. The witnesses' statements tell you who stood where, who was beside which object, and who shared a room.

Every case has exactly one answer, and it can always be reached by reasoning from the first move. No guessing is needed.

In the first six chapters the witnesses tell the truth. Place a person wrongly and the haunting spreads, so think before you commit. In the final chapter, two of the speakers are lying.
