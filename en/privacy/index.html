The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information about a visit, such as IP address, browser type and the page requested, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. That processing is GitHub's, not a tracker we added.

Email you send us

If you write to mingxufortune@gmail.com, or to mengjingchanyu@gmail.com from the Chalkdoku support page, we receive the address you write from, your message and any files you attach. We use that to answer you and to look into the problem. We do not add you to a marketing list. Please do not send passwords or payment details.

Our mail provider, Google, processes that correspondence under the Google Privacy Policy. Mail may be handled outside your country, including in the United States.

How long we keep email

We keep a support message only as long as we need it to answer you and fix the issue, then delete it. A visit to this website is not stored by us, because the pages themselves keep no accounts or logs of their own.

Changes

If the way this website handles visits or support email changes, we will update this page and the date above.
